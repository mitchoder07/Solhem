01 · The practices

Four ways to slow down.

Sunrise

Vinyasa flow

A steady morning flow to wake the spine. Sun salutations, standing poses, one honest challenge, and a long cool down.

60 min · all levels · sunrise

Candlelit

Yin stillness

Five quiet minutes per shape. Works the connective tissue, settles the nervous system, and ends in time for bed.

75 min · gentle · evening

Bolstered

Restorative rest

Bolsters, blankets, and nowhere to be. Every pose is propped so the body can fully let go. The hardest part is staying awake.

60 min · very gentle · any time

Seated

Breathwork

Twenty minutes of guided breathing that feels like a nap and works like a reset. No poses, no experience, no flexibility needed.

30 min · seated · lunchtime
